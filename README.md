# ZU World

A smart indoor campus map for Zayed University, built as a capstone prototype by Khalifa Rashid.

**Live demo:** https://khalifarashid.github.io/zu-world/

## What it does
- Real ZU Abu Dhabi layout: female, central and male campuses, gates 1–10 with entry/exit rules, car parks
- Zoomable, searchable map of every classroom, office, lab, restroom and service on campus
- Live room status: white = free, red = class in session
- Step-by-step walking routes ("Take me there")
- "Free rooms" list sorted by how long each room stays free
- Doctor-to-student office ping that opens directions
- Demo clock to show rooms changing through the day

## Note
Buildings, promenades, gates and car parks follow the ZU Abu Dhabi campus plan. Room layouts, doctor names and the timetable are sample data, not Zayed University's real schedule.
